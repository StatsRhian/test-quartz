---
title: Productivity red flags
---

When I spot myself doing these things, it's usually a sign that I'm avoiding something or I don't have a good place to deal with it

### Keeping a file in my `Downloads/`

 Files should be on GitHub, or a work file store. I don't keep anything locally. If something is in my `Downloads/` it should be deleteable. So if it's sticking around that's a sign something has gone wrong

### Repeatedly snoozing an email

I try to keep inbox zero, and I love snoozing an email to get it out of my mind. However, if I've snoozed an email a couple of times it probabaly means I don't know how to approach it, or I'm avoiding it.

### Cancelling a meeting

When work feels stressful, my instinct is "I can't possibly have time to go to the lucnh & learn" or "It would be better to postpone my 1:1 until next when I'll have more time". Sometimes on a really tight, important deadling, this is the right approach. However, often when I get the urge to cancel a meeting it's because I'm overwhelmed and it's best to step back and solve the workload problem first.
